**Description**

> Hedgeroll is a cozy drawing puzzle. A hedgehog waits at the top of the forest, and his stump house is somewhere below. Draw vines with your finger, tap Roll, and watch him tumble down the path you made.
>
> Every level gives you a limited amount of vine, so plan each line. Bridge ponds, slip between brambles, bounce off springy purple mushrooms and ride warm updrafts. Collect all three berries on a level for a perfect finish.
>
> • 12 hand-built levels across three worlds: Morning Glade, Amber Hollow and Firefly Night
> • Real rolling physics with slopes, bounces, rocks, wind and water
> • Undo and redraw as often as you like
> • No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
> • Plays fully offline, and your progress saves on your device

**App Review notes**

> A drawing puzzle that runs fully offline. There is no login, no network use and no data collection. Draw a vine below the hedgehog, then tap Roll.
