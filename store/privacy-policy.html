What we collect

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. They don't connect to the internet while you play, and they don't send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The games save a few things on your own phone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Hedgeroll: which levels you've finished and how many berries you collected on each.
  • Hedgehop: your best height.
  • Both: whether sound is on or off.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no personal information, they collect none from children either.
