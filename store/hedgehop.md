**Promotional text** [161/170]

> Bounce a hedgehog up through the forest. Mushroom caps launch him sky-high, dandelions float him up, and wasps pop when you land on them. How high can you climb?

**Description**

> Hedgehop is an endless bouncing climb. The hedgehog bounces on his own. You steer him left and right from branch to branch and see how high he can go.
>
> The forest changes as you rise, from the sunny Morning Glade to the autumn Amber Hollow and up into Firefly Night. The higher you go, the trickier it gets.
>
> • Mushroom caps launch you high into the air
> • Dry twig bundles snap after one bounce
> • Leaf rafts drift from side to side
> • Dandelions carry you straight up
> • Updrafts give you a free lift
> • Steer around brambles, and land on wasps from above to pop them
> • Run off one side of the screen to pop out the other
> • Collect berries and beat your best height
> • No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
> • Plays fully offline

**App Review notes**

> An endless bouncing game that runs fully offline. There is no login, no network use and no data collection. Tap Play, then hold the left or right side of the screen to steer.
